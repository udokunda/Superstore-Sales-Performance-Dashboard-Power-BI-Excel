# Superstore-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Power-BI-Excel
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales performance using the Superstore dataset. The objective was to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development

# Business Objectives
The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which product categories contribute most to revenue?
- How do sales and profits perform over time?
- Which products and segments drive business performance?
- What trends and patterns can support better decision-making?

# Tools Used
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard development
DAX – KPI and business metric calculations

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin %
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive filters (Region, Category, Segment, Date)
- Sales and Profit trend analysis
- Regional and Category performance analysis
- KPI cards for business performance tracking
- Dynamic and interactive visualizations

# Data Cleaning Process
The raw dataset contained inconsistencies that required preprocessing before analysis.
## Cleaning Steps
- Imported the dataset into Excel.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Standardized date formats.
- Identified and removed unnecessary null values.
- Validated numerical fields.
- Ensured data consistency before importing into Power BI.

# Key Findings
## 1. Sales Performance
- Sales were concentrated within a few high-performing regions, indicating strong geographic demand patterns.
## 2. Product Categories
- Certain categories generated significantly higher revenue than others, contributing heavily to overall business performance.
## 3. Profitability
-High sales volume did not always translate into high profitability, highlighting the importance of analyzing profit alongside revenue.
## 4. Seasonal Trends
- Sales exhibited fluctuations throughout the year, suggesting periods of increased customer demand.
## 5. Customer Segments
- Some customer segments contributed disproportionately to total sales and profit, presenting opportunities for targeted business strategies.
